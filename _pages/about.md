Hi, there! I am a second-year PhD candidate at <a href="https://github.com/tmlr-group">TMLR Group</a> in the <a href="https://eng.unimelb.edu.au/">Faculty of Engineering and Information Technology</a>, <a href="https://www.unimelb.edu.au/">the University of Melbourne</a>, advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=eqe3JS8AAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao">Dr. Feng Liu</a> and <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/liuhua-peng">Dr. Liuhua Peng</a>. I received my Master's degree from the <a href="https://ai.nju.edu.cn/">School of Artificial Intelligence</a> at <a href="https://www.nju.edu.cn/en/">Nanjing University</a> in 2024, where I was supervised by <a href="https://www.lamda.nju.edu.cn/gaow/">Dr. Wei Gao</a> and was a member of the <a href="https://www.lamda.nju.edu.cn/MainPage.ashx">LAMDA Group</a>, led by Professor <a href="https://cs.nju.edu.cn/zhouzh/">Zhihua Zhou</a>. Prior to that, I received my B.Eng. degree from <a href="https://en.dlut.edu.cn/">Dalian University of Technology</a> in 2021. 

My research interests lie at the intersection of <strong>statistics</strong> and <strong>trustworthy AI</strong>. At a general level, I am interested in <strong>data-adaptive statistical hypothesis testing</strong>, which provides a foundation for statistical guarantees, and in <strong>statistically rigorous methods for evaluation and trustworthiness assessment</strong> in modern AI systems. Specifically, my current research focuses on:
<ul>
  <li>
    LLM evaluation with fewer labeled samples while maintaining finite-sample validity and provable error control.
  </li>
  <li>
    Learnable hypothesis testing frameworks based on data, rather than relying on hand-crafted test statistics and pre-specified procedures, to address modern machine learning tasks with statistical guarantees.
  </li>
</ul>
